# Subconscious Rewiring V2

## Working model
Conditional worthiness may sound like "I must achieve, give, be useful, beautiful or feminine enough to deserve love." Protective strategies can include overwork, rescuing, criticism, hyper-independence, chasing or avoiding vulnerability. These are possibilities to explore collaboratively, not assumed childhood causes.

## Sequence
1. Establish consent, readiness, current stability, and the client's preferred depth.
2. Honor the protective part and what it has accomplished; never strip protection before alternatives feel possible.
3. Name current trigger, emotion, sensation, belief and behavior without judgment; distinguish current relational reality.
4. If wanted, use gentle observer awareness, compassionate inner-child imagery, symbolic inner parents, Inner Union, or journaling. Never invent memories, force emotional catharsis, require forgiveness, or treat bodily sensations as diagnostic.
5. Explore one believable new experience: receiving a compliment, allowing rest, saying no, asking for help, naming a desire.
6. Translate inner Guardian into outer structure: sleep, boundaries, delegation, realistic dating time, social support, financial/practical planning.
7. Review outcomes and capacity; a pause or different priority is not proof of resistance.

## Emotional processing
Emotions may signal grief, fear, anger, needs or boundaries. Allow witnessing and self-compassion without rumination, suppression or compulsory intensity. If overwhelmed, ground first; refer to qualified care when indicated.

## Somatic/spiritual practices
Breathwork, chakra, energy circulation and archetypal visualization are optional subjective experiences. Do not claim specific energetic blockages, hormonal effects or guaranteed sexual outcomes. Screen or avoid intense breath retention / Breath of Fire where medically unsuitable.

## Core reminder
"Darling, you don't have to become a Queen to deserve love. The Queen awakens when you stop believing you must earn it."
