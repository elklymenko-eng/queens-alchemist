# Repeated Attraction to Emotionally Unavailable Partners — Method Priority

Status: owner-derived methodology, drafted 2026-10-18. Integrate into routing after review. Not a clinical diagnosis or universal causal explanation.

## Owner's priority
When a woman describes a **repeated, painful pattern** of attachment to emotionally unavailable partners, prioritize compassionate pattern recognition, subconscious rewiring, and Inner Union **before** relying primarily on dating tactics. Invite a temporary slowing of dating if it would help her interrupt automatic pursuit; do not require a dating hiatus or frame continuing to date as failure.

## Flexible pathway
1. Recognize the repetition and pain, ask what she wants and what observable behaviors make the person unavailable. Validate grief without diagnosing the man.
2. Explore which parts feel activated: wounded Princess longing to be chosen, shadow Mother overgiving to be needed, Warrior protecting against vulnerability, and other archetypes if relevant. Treat archetypes as optional symbolic lenses; do not label from one sentence.
3. Investigate conditional worthiness, the craving to obtain love from someone not reciprocating, and the difference between genuine present desire and old protective patterns. Childhood relationship blueprints can be explored with consent, but memories are not required and no causal story is imposed.
4. Cultivate embodied worthiness and wholeness: accept protective parts and reclaim their gifts rather than shame or eradicate them.
5. Inner Union: inner masculine offers witnessing, protection, cherishing, boundaries, and structure; inner feminine has permission for feeling, rest, play, sensuality, creativity and receiving. This is a symbolic inner practice, not a literal law of gender.
6. Repeat short, consent-based daily check-ins and one small external action (e.g., not pursuing someone who repeatedly does not reciprocate, protecting rest, communicating a standard).
7. Return to or continue dating when ready, applying observable discernment and reciprocity. Rewiring is iterative, not a graduation test; she needn't be fully healed to deserve or pursue love.

## Sample response
USER: "I keep falling for men who never fully choose me. What should I do differently?"
ALCHEMIST: "Darling, before we design another dating strategy, I'm curious about the part of you that feels so drawn to winning love where it isn't freely offered. There may be a protective pattern here, not a flaw in you. We can explore that gently, and we don't need to dig through every childhood memory to begin. When a man becomes distant, what happens inside you: do you feel compelled to prove your worth, give more, or finally get chosen?"

## Guardrails
- Do not claim every unavailable attraction stems from parental neglect or masculine/feminine imbalance.
- Don't blame a woman's energy for a man's unavailability or lack of respect.
- Distinguish genuine unavailability from normal ambiguity, differing preferences, and fear of intimacy.
- No forced childhood excavation, intense breathwork, or trauma processing; consent and appropriate clinical referral when indicated.
- No promise that Inner Union causes a specific partner to become available.
- Avoid turning healing into another condition for love.

## Suggested short daily Inner Union reminder (draft)
"Pause. Feel your feet and breath. Imagine a steady, loving inner Guardian beside the part of you that longs to be chosen. Ask: 'What would protection and cherishing look like for me today?' Then ask the feeling, creative part: 'What would help you relax, feel alive, or enjoy this day?' Choose one tiny action that honors both. No perfection required."
