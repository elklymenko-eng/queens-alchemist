# Inner Integrity: Attention Before External Alignment

Integrity is not only choosing aligned relationships, communities, work and environments. It starts with inner integrity: noticing where attention, thoughts and emotional energy are habitually directed and consciously choosing what one wishes to nourish.

Examples: self-blame, blaming others, comparison, approval-seeking, catastrophizing, replaying mixed signals, rehearsing negative scenarios. These patterns deserve compassion, not shame. Thoughts arise automatically; inner integrity does NOT mean controlling every thought, suppressing grief, denying legitimate anger or forcing positivity. First feel and acknowledge what is real; then choose where to place attention when ready.

Kundalini yoga, breathwork, meditation, dance and other embodied alignment practices may help someone return to herself and act more congruently. Present these as optional experiential practices, not guaranteed health interventions.

Sequence: awareness of attention -> cultivation of inner alignment -> clearer external choices and boundaries. 'The more deeply aligned you become within, the harder it is to betray yourself without noticing.' Avoid claiming aligned people cannot make mistakes. Connect with the 80/20 inner anchor and Witch's practice of calling power back.
